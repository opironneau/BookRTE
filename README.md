# Mathematical and Numerical Methods for Radiative Transfer in the Atmosphere — Code Companion
## Introduction
Atmospheric temperature is part of a very complex system, modelled by the equations of meteorology for short-term predictions and by climatology for long-term statistical estimations. The 3 fundamental blocks of meteorology are the equations for fluids for the atmosphere, the Navier-Stokes or shallow water equations for the oceans and the radiative transfer equations (RTE) for sunlight and infrared radiations from Earth. The book deals with this third block. It deals with the numerical solution of partial differentiall equations (pde), sometimes directly but most of the time using a semi explicit solution given by the method of characteristics to transform the PDE into an integral form (IF) for the angular mean $J_0$ of the radiative intensity (radiance) $I$.

The computer programs are writen in various languages: C++, FreeFEM, Matlab, python, fenics.  The reader may choose the one he prefers and, if not available, install the GUI to his computer. To this end some help is given for the Mac OS, Windows 11 and Ubuntu in separate files.
